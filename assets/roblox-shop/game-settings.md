# Roblox Game Settings

Use these values in Roblox Creator Dashboard > Experience Details.

## Recommended Name

Galactic Animal Worlds

## Short Name Options

- Galactic Animal Worlds
- Galactic Dino Worlds
- Catch & Ride Galaxy

## Description

Ride a galactic skateboard, unlock colorful worlds, catch birds and dinos, ride giant dinosaurs, eat cherries from tall trees, and capture glowing fruit in a huge adventure map.

Clear each stage to open the next zone:

- Playground and pizza battles
- Galactic station
- Jungle animals
- Sports, tennis, and game station worlds
- Coffee shop, burger house, pizza place, and swimming pool bird-catching worlds
- Dinosaur park, dino beach, flying dino forest, cherry grove, and galactic orchard

Collect cash, buy speed and jump boosts, spin the prize wheel, travel through time machines, and keep pushing forward to unlock every world.

## One-Line Tagline

Catch animals, ride dinos, unlock worlds, and collect galactic fruit.

## Genre

Adventure / Obby / Simulator

## Suggested Tags

Adventure, Animals, Dinosaurs, Birds, Simulator, Obby, Skateboard, Galaxy, Worlds, Collect

## Game Icon Direction

Main icon should show:

- A happy player on a neon galactic skateboard
- A friendly giant dinosaur head behind the player
- A glowing bird and galactic fruit near the top corners
- A colorful portal/world gate in the background
- Big readable title text: `GALACTIC ANIMAL WORLDS`

Recommended format:

- 512x512 PNG
- Bright, readable, high contrast
- Simple shapes that stay clear on mobile
- Avoid tiny text, clutter, dark-only backgrounds, or copyrighted characters
